Reitmans return policy: a price ending in $.97 is final sale

At Reitmans, an item bought in store with a price ending in $.97 can't be returned or exchanged. Everything else gets 30 days, unworn, unwashed, tags on, with the receipt. For the holidays, purchases made from October 9 to December 5, 2026 can be returned until January 4, 2027; after December 5, it's 30 days.
